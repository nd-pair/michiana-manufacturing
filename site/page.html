Michiana Manufacturing

The manufacturing and technology companies of the South Bend–Elkhart region, on one map. Each column stands at a company's address; its height is headcount and its color the sector. Pan, zoom from the Chicago–Cleveland span down to the campus, and filter by what companies make.

Every column is a company at its street address; height is headcount on a log scale, color is the sector. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom toward the cursor, hover a column for its name and click it for detail. Click a sector or product bar to filter the map and the roster.

Add or correct a company

Every company is one short text file in the companies folder of the repository: name, address, sector, products and headcount. Edit a file, or add a new one from the template in the README, and the map rebuilds itself when the change is merged. Only public information belongs here: what a company says about itself on its website, in filings, press and business directories.
